cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players each on a large, oval-shaped grass field 
22-yard-long strip of hard-packed earth or turf known as the pitch
The core objective of the game is for one team to score more runs (points) than the opposing team. The game alternates through sections called innings, where one team bats to score runs while the other team bowls and fields to stop them and get them "out".

Did you know

The first ever official international cricket match was played between the United States and Canada in 1844.
